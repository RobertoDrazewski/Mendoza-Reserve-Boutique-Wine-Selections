    A shorter route between the winery and the glass.

    Mendoza Reserve is a boutique wine broker. We connect independent Mendoza wineries with UK restaurants and importers who want wines they cannot find through the usual distribution.

    Direct from the estate
    Each order goes to the winery that made the wine. The winery invoices the balance and arranges shipping.
    A curated list
    Wineries join after we contact them and confirm their participation, so every entry on the list is active.
    One point of contact
    Questions on allocation, availability or logistics come to us, in English, on UK time.

    Request trade access

    Tell us what you are looking for. We reply within one working day with the wineries that fit.
